1 · On a phone

Three separate complaints, three separate answers, and you can see them fight each other here: text too small, having to zoom, and pop-ups that swallow the screen. The book-shelf app answers the first two with a Simple switch and an A− / A+ size remembered per device. Drag the size up and watch the number under the phone: that is how much width the layout actually has left, which is why things start overlapping long before you'd expect.

2 · The decorated edge

The overlap you're seeing isn't random. Every emoji is placed against the whole window, and the top row sits at 4% down and 3% in — about 36 by 43 pixels, which is exactly where the app's name and tabs are. Below, As it is now reproduces that; the rest are the ways out. I've already committed the first fix (the emoji get their own layer that begins under the header), but the look is yours to choose.

3 · The background

Two things are being asked here and they pull apart, so they're two rows of controls. Each swatch carries a real card off your own shelf, so you can see whether the writing survives the prettier ones — that's the whole reason the pale rule exists, and it's worth breaking with your eyes open rather than by accident.
